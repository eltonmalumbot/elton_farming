# Evergreen Life 🌾

A cozy, original isometric farming and life simulation browser game, inspired by the genres of farming and life simulation.

## Game

- Plant, water and harvest crops on an isometric grid
- Grow relationships with NPCs
- Maintain energy, hunger, hygiene and social needs
- Decorate your home and shop for seeds
- Save progress automatically to your browser

Play using the Vercel deployment linked to this repository. Controls: mouse/touch to select and interact with tiles; WASD or arrow keys for keyboard movement.

## Development

The deployable prototype is a self-contained static HTML5/Canvas app at `index.html`. Open it directly in a browser. No dependencies or environment variables are required for v0.1.

Future roadmap: convert to a modular engine (Phaser + React), better art and animation, livestock, seasons, NPC schedules, house builder and family simulation.

Original project: no Harvest Moon or The Sims assets or copyrighted content are included.
